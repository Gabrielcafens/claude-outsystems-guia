NOTES

Why one at a time: a Script and a Block sent in the same turn exhausted the
daily quota with OS-AISA-42903 and published nothing at all, neither of them.
Split into two turns, both passed. Three quota exhaustions in three days, every
one on a turn that asked for several things at once; none on a turn that asked
for one.

Why "nothing that is not listed above": helpfulness is the failure mode. Ask
for a Block and Mentor will often add the screen that uses it, wire the two
together, and mention it in passing. Now two things changed in a turn you
wanted to be able to undo, and the report reads as success.

Why "do not publish": publishing is the irreversible part of the loop.
Everything before it can be thrown away.

Why the report: Mentor builds STRUCTURE exactly - Blocks, parameters, events,
handlers, screens all came back as specified. It rewrites CONTENT. Two measured
cases, both after an explicit instruction to copy literally: an escape sequence
inside a CSS comment changed character, and a column-configuration JSON came
back with labels changed, columns reordered and keys translated. The JSON was
still valid, so nothing failed loudly - the badge colours just silently stopped
matching the data.

So: model through Mentor, payload by hand. Anything over about 100 KB does not
merely change, it fails: a 106 KB stylesheet killed a turn with OS-AIWA-50001.
Pasting the same file into ODC Studio is instant and costs no quota.

If the turn fails, resume that session - do not start a new one. See the
OutSystems Mentor instruction for the rest.
