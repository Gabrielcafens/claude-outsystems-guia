-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NOTES

Why "do not edit the original": a variant beside the original is additive and
reviewable. An edited original changes every screen already using it, usually
without anyone noticing until someone else's page looks wrong.

Why "stop and ask": an unanswered design question does not disappear when an
agent picks one of the options. It becomes a decision nobody made, buried in
code, that someone has to reverse-engineer later.
